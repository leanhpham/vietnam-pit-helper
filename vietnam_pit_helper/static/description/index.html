Vietnam PIT Helper

Monthly payroll & Vietnamese personal income tax (PIT) — Odoo 19 Community — $19 one-time

Enter gross salary, bonus and number of dependents for each employee and month. The module automatically computes compulsory insurance, family deductions, taxable income, PIT and net salary under the Vietnamese tax law in force for tax year 2026 (Law 109/2025/QH15) — the current 5-bracket progressive table, not the pre-2026 law.

What it computes (2026 law)

StepRule
Insurance (employee)BHXH 8%, BHYT 1.5%, BHTN 1% of gross salary. Caps: BHXH/BHYT base max 50,600,000 VND/month (20× base salary 2,530,000); BHTN base max 106,200,000 VND/month (20× Region I minimum wage 5,310,000).
Family deduction15,500,000 VND/month (personal) + 6,200,000 VND/month per dependent.
Taxable incomeGross + bonus − insurance − family deduction (floored at 0).
PITProgressive monthly table, 5 brackets: ≤10M 5%, 10–30M 10%, 30–60M 20%, 60–100M 30%, >100M 35%.
Net salaryGross + bonus − insurance − PIT.

Worked example (verified on a live Odoo 19 Community install)

Employee B, December 2026: gross 25,000,000 VND, bonus 3,000,000 VND, 1 dependent → insurance 2,625,000 VND, taxable income 3,675,000 VND, PIT 183,750 VND, net salary 25,191,250 VND. The same figures appear in the screenshots and workflow video for this version.

Features

  • One payroll line per employee per month (unique constraint — no duplicates).
  • Draft → Confirmed → Done workflow; amounts are stored and searchable.
  • List and form views with filters by month and employee; totals in the list footer.
  • QWeb report: monthly payroll + PIT table, printable / exportable to PDF.
  • Access control: salary figures visible only to HR and Accounting groups. Other users cannot see the model at all; Accounting is read-only.
  • No Enterprise dependency — Odoo 19 Community only; depends on the standard hr (Employees) and account (Accounting) apps.

Install

  1. Copy the vietnam_pit_helper folder into your Odoo addons path.
  2. In Odoo: Apps → Update Apps List → search “Vietnam PIT Helper” → Install. (Requires the Employees and Accounting apps.)
  3. Menu: Employees → Payroll & PIT.

Out of scope (v1)

Year-end settlement, eTax filing and electronic declaration are not included in this version.
